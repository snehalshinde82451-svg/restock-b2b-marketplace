About ReStock

ReStock is a prototype concept designed to make bulk sourcing easier for small businesses.

It focuses on convenience, easy price comparison and simpler supplier discovery. It is a hackathon demo, not a live business.

Privacy Policy (Demo Example)

This is an example page for a prototype, not legal advice. Policy text is shown in English only.

ReStock is a hackathon prototype. It uses Supabase for sign-in and data storage when configured (otherwise a local demo mode), and has no payments.

Accounts, product listings, quote requests and bulk orders are stored in the project’s Supabase database and are visible only to the parties involved (buyer and supplier). In local demo mode they stay in your browser.

Please use sample information only. You can delete everything at any time with the “Reset demo data” button on the dashboard, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Terms of Use (Demo Example)

This is an example page for a prototype, not legal advice. Policy text is shown in English only.

All products, prices, suppliers and locations on ReStock are fictional sample data and do not represent real offers. No supplier on this site has been verified.

Quotes and bulk orders are recorded between accounts for demonstration; no payments or binding contracts are made through this prototype.

The prototype is provided as-is for demonstration and learning purposes.
